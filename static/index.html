If you are in immediate danger

Contact your local emergency number or go to the nearest emergency department. If possible, stay with someone you trust.

☏

Professional support

A qualified mental-health professional can help you understand what you are experiencing and discuss appropriate support.

Important

MindMirror cannot monitor emergencies, assess immediate risk or contact emergency services for you. Do not rely on this app when urgent human help is needed.
